# IT Risk Assessment Workbook

A complete, step-by-step **IT risk assessment** in one Excel workbook — from discovery to a treatment plan.

📥 **Workbook:** [IT_Risk_Assessment_Workbook.xlsx](IT_Risk_Assessment_Workbook.xlsx)

## What's inside (9 sheets, in order)

| # | Sheet | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Discovery | Business context, scope and key systems |
| 2 | Legal Requirements | Laws, regulations and contractual obligations in scope |
| 3 | Asset Inventory | Critical IT assets and their owners |
| 4 | Probability Matrix | Likelihood rating scale |
| 5 | Impact Matrix | Business impact rating scale |
| 6 | Risk Register | Identified risks scored for likelihood × impact |
| 7 | Heat Map | Visual view of risk concentration |
| 8 | Risk Dashboard | Summary metrics for leadership |
| 9 | Treatment Plan | Mitigate / transfer / accept / avoid decisions with owners and dates |

## Skills shown
Risk identification · qualitative risk scoring · risk registers · heat maps · risk treatment planning · executive reporting
